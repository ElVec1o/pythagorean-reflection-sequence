Two seats (Ramanujan, Erd\H os), each trying a route independent of the $\rho$-type criteria
already exhausted in \texttt{P6\_note.tex}/\texttt{P6\_v2\_note.tex}.

\textbf{Ramanujan: the regular continued fraction of $q^\ast$ itself looks generic.} All $999$
partial quotients were computed and certified against both endpoints of the $2090$-digit bracket
(they agree throughout, so the sequence is correct regardless of where $q^\ast$ sits inside the
bracket). The maximum quotient ($8250$ at index $170$) is a normal heavy-tailed Gauss--Kuzmin
fluctuation at this length, not a Liouville-type jump; the digit-frequency histogram matches the
Gauss--Kuzmin law and the geometric mean ($2.7366$) is close to Khinchin's constant ($2.68545$); an
exhaustive length-5 window scan found no periodicity or near-periodicity (which would have proved
quadratic irrationality via Lagrange's theorem, an exciting but a priori extremely unlikely
outcome). \textbf{Verdict: genuine, but uninformative} -- it closes off the one elementary
Diophantine-approximation avenue not already covered, without being able to prove or disprove
irrationality itself (a generic-looking CF is consistent with irrational, transcendental, or even
a ``boring'' algebraic number of no special approximation quality).

\textbf{Erd\H os: wider PSLQ search against Gamma values, $q$-Pochhammer/eta-type constants,
Bessel zeros, and algebraic self-combinations of $\beta_2$ -- nothing found.} All searches returned
no relation, except one which the seat itself correctly identified as spurious (a tautology from
including $\beta_2-1$ in its own search basis) and explicitly flagged as such rather than reporting
a false discovery.

\textbf{Room verdict: NO PROGRESS,} honestly, with two more elementary approaches closed off for
precise reasons (generic CF; no exotic-constant relation found) rather than left untried. P6 stands
exactly where \texttt{P6\_note.tex}/\texttt{P6\_v2\_note.tex} left it (v10.21.0): $\beta_2$ is
proved not to be a root of any integer polynomial of degree $\le64$; irrationality and
transcendence remain OPEN.
