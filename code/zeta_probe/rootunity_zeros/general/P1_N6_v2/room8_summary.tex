\textbf{Hilbert: INCONCLUSIVE, honestly and usefully.} The 5 angular loci from Room 1's ray-lemma
analysis cannot literally be checked, because no N=6 analogue of \texttt{prop:qi}'s $\Gamma_\pm$,
$\Pi_0$ contours has ever been constructed in this repository -- only the $N=4$ ($q=i$) version
exists. Reading the $N=4$ template directly (rather than guessing) revealed a real structural
escape pattern that might transfer: the angular ray-lemma mechanism is only invoked on bounded

is instead certified via an angle-free modulus bound immune to resonance loci entirely. Whether
this transfers to a hypothetical $N=6$ construction is unknown; building that construction is
substantial new work, not attempted here. Confirmed explicitly: even a fully favorable resolution
of this piece would \emph{not} close $N=6$ by itself (the $N=6$-specific landscape bookkeeping is
still missing), and this piece is independent of Ramanujan's separately-confirmed doomed
crude-method margin (different method entirely).

stated precisely. \textbf{Both zeros are now fully rigorously certified}, upgrading them from ``one
notch below full rigor'' (Room 1) to a genuine argument-principle certificate.

\textbf{Room verdict: ADVANCED.} A small, genuine, now-rigorous closure (2 certified zeros of $B$
