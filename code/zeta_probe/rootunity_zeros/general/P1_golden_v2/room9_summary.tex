Two seats pushed on closing the golden-threshold gap directly.

\textbf{Tao: genuine Weyl differencing attempted, AMPLITUDE FAILS, and the mechanism is now shown
to be the SAME ONE found independently in P2.} $\psi_n=[Y^n]\exp(\Phi_q(Y))$ with
$\Phi_q(Y)=\sum_{m\ge1,\,q\nmid m}u^mY^m/(m(1-\zeta_0^{-m}))$ is not slowly varying: the ratio
$|\psi_{n+1}/\psi_n|$ jumps $1.6$--$4.5\times$ exactly at every $n\equiv0\pmod q$, riding on an
otherwise smooth envelope. Weyl differencing correctly reduces the quadratic phase to a linear one
after one step (the phase step works exactly as advertised), but the resulting Abel-summation bound
needs the total variation of the shifted amplitude to be small relative to its sum; instead it is
dominated by the same periodic $O(1)$-relative spikes, so no net cancellation survives past the
square root. \textbf{This is mechanistically the same obstruction Tao's seat found independently
for P2's $w_\rho=c^\rho/(\zeta;\zeta)_{2\rho}$}: both amplitudes are built from $1-\zeta^{(\cdot)}$
factors that are never uniformly slowly varying modulo the relevant period, and in this problem
$\Phi_q$ is literally $\log$ of a $q$-Pochhammer-type product excluding $q\mid m$, so exponentiating
it convolves the near-resonance at $m\equiv0\pmod q$ into every coefficient. A real, unifying
diagnosis across two of this session's hardest problems, not a coincidence.

\textbf{Erd\H os: 12 genuine rigorous point-certificates deep in the previously-unreachable
window.} Three alternative routes (a modular-transform reciprocity, a weaker dense-subset
statement, a Jensen/Rouch\'e interpolation-in-$\ell$ argument) were examined and set aside with
stated reasons -- none constructed. A fourth, more direct idea worked: using the already-existing
rigorous exact-formula tool (\texttt{P1e\_logderiv.py}, Arb ball arithmetic, no crude bound
anywhere), 12 explicit $(a,N)$ points were located and certified throughout $\ell\in(0.082,0.385)$
-- well inside the window the crude method cannot reach -- with $N$ up to $701$. Every one gives a
genuine, machine-verified lower bound on $|Z_N(a/N)|$, e.g.\ $44/503$ ($\ell=0.082$,
$|Z|\ge1.528$), $68/701$ ($\ell=0.183$, $|Z|\ge0.987$), $48/401$ ($\ell=0.385$, $|Z|\ge0.415$).
These are narrow (individual points, not a density statement) but entirely genuine, analogous to
P2's individual certified zeros.

\textbf{Room verdict: ADVANCED.} No general theorem for the window and no route to one was found
that survives scrutiny -- but real content survives: (a) 12 genuine non-vanishing certificates
deep in the hard window, narrow but citable; (b) a precise, unifying mechanistic diagnosis
connecting this problem's amplitude obstruction to P2's independently-discovered one, suggesting
both hard problems in this research program share a common root cause (near-resonant
$1-\zeta^{(\cdot)}$-type amplitudes defeating standard analytic-number-theory smoothing
techniques). The golden threshold $\ell\approx0.484$ remains the boundary of what the general
method proves; the window below it is not empty of results, just not yet covered by a theorem.
